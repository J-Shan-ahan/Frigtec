Transitioning to Lower-GWP Refrigerants

What are Lower-GWP Refrigerants?

GWP (Global Warming Potential) measures how much a refrigerant contributes to global warming compared to carbon dioxide. Lower-GWP refrigerants have significantly less impact on climate change while maintaining excellent cooling performance.

Industry Transition

The HVAC&R industry is actively transitioning away from high-GWP refrigerants like R404A and R134a toward more environmentally friendly alternatives such as R32, R290 (propane), R744 (CO₂), and other natural refrigerants.

Future-Focused Solutions

FRIGTEC stays informed about the latest refrigerant technologies and regulations. We can help you understand your options and plan for future refrigerant transitions, ensuring your systems remain compliant and efficient.

Retrofit & Upgrade Options

If you're operating older systems with high-GWP refrigerants, we can assess your equipment and recommend retrofit or upgrade options that reduce environmental impact while maintaining reliable performance.
