Complete HVAC-R Servicing

FrigTec provides comprehensive HVAC-R (Heating, Ventilation, Air Conditioning, and Refrigeration) servicing for all types of commercial equipment. Our experienced technicians service, repair, and optimize your systems to ensure peak performance and energy efficiency.

Full-Service HVAC-R Solutions

Whether you need routine servicing, system upgrades, or performance optimization, our team has the expertise to handle all aspects of commercial HVAC-R systems. We work with all major brands and equipment types.

Equipment We Service

  • Commercial Refrigeration - Walk-in coolrooms, freezers, display cabinets, and cold storage
  • Air Conditioning Systems - Split systems, ducted units, VRV/VRF, and package units
  • Ice Machines - Commercial ice makers and ice storage systems
  • Ventilation Systems - Exhaust fans, fresh air systems, and air handling units
  • Refrigerated Transport - Truck and van refrigeration units
  • Chillers & Condensing Units - Water-cooled and air-cooled systems

Our Service Capabilities

  • Routine Servicing - Scheduled maintenance to keep systems running efficiently
  • Repairs & Troubleshooting - Expert diagnosis and repair of all issues
  • System Upgrades - Modernization and efficiency improvements
  • Performance Optimization - Fine-tuning for maximum efficiency
  • Refrigerant Management - Leak detection, recovery, and recharging
  • Compliance Testing - Ensuring systems meet Australian standards
  • Energy Audits - Identifying opportunities to reduce operating costs

Service Benefits
