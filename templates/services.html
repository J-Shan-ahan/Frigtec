Preventative Maintenance Programs

Regular maintenance is essential for keeping your refrigeration and HVAC systems running efficiently and preventing costly breakdowns. Our comprehensive maintenance programs are designed to extend equipment life, reduce energy consumption, and ensure food safety compliance.

What's Included

  • Scheduled Inspections - Regular system checks and performance monitoring
  • Filter Cleaning & Replacement - Maintaining optimal airflow and efficiency
  • Refrigerant Level Checks - Ensuring proper cooling capacity

  • Condenser & Evaporator Cleaning - Maximizing heat transfer efficiency
  • Thermostat Calibration - Accurate temperature control
  • Door Seal Inspection - Preventing energy loss
  • Detailed Service Reports - Complete documentation of all work performed

Maintenance Plans

{% elif service.title == 'Break down repairs' %}

24/7 Emergency Repair Service

When your refrigeration system fails, every minute counts. Our emergency breakdown service provides rapid response to minimize product loss and business disruption. We understand the critical nature of commercial refrigeration and prioritize urgent repairs.

Common Repairs We Handle

  • Compressor Failures - Diagnosis and replacement of faulty compressors
  • Refrigerant Leaks - Leak detection, repair, and recharging
  • Electrical Issues - Control board repairs and wiring problems
  • Temperature Control Problems - Thermostat and sensor replacement
  • Fan Motor Failures - Condenser and evaporator fan repairs
  • Door & Seal Issues - Gasket replacement and door adjustments
  • Defrost System Faults - Timer and heater element repairs
  • Ice Build-up Problems - Drainage and airflow corrections

Our Repair Process

We arrive equipped with a comprehensive range of parts and diagnostic tools to resolve most issues on the first visit. Our technicians quickly identify the problem, provide a clear explanation and quote, and complete repairs efficiently to get your system back online.

Emergency Response

  • Fast response times - typically within 2-4 hours
  • Fully stocked service vehicles
  • Experienced technicians available 24/7
  • Upfront pricing with no hidden costs
  • Warranty on all repair work
  • Temporary cooling solutions if needed

Service Areas

We provide emergency breakdown services throughout Brisbane, Gold Coast, and surrounding areas. Contact us immediately if you're experiencing refrigeration issues.
