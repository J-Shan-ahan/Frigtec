Emergency Breakdown Repairs

24/7 emergency repair service for refrigeration and HVAC systems

24/7 Emergency Breakdown Service

When your refrigeration or air conditioning system fails, every minute counts. FrigTec provides rapid emergency response to minimize product loss and business disruption. Our experienced technicians are available 24/7 to get your systems back online quickly.

Fast Emergency Response

We understand the critical nature of commercial refrigeration and HVAC systems. Our emergency service provides rapid response - typically within 1-2 hours - to diagnose and repair your system. We arrive fully equipped to handle most repairs on the first visit.

Common Emergency Repairs We Handle

  • Compressor Failures - Complete diagnosis and replacement of faulty compressors
  • Refrigerant Leaks - Leak detection, repair, and system recharging
  • Electrical Failures - Control board repairs and wiring issues
  • Temperature Control Problems - Thermostat and sensor replacement
  • Fan Motor Failures - Condenser and evaporator fan repairs
  • Door & Seal Issues - Emergency gasket replacement and door adjustments
  • Defrost System Faults - Timer and heater element repairs
  • Ice Build-up Problems - Drainage and airflow corrections
  • Complete System Failures - Comprehensive diagnostics and repairs

Our Emergency Repair Process

When you call, we dispatch a technician immediately. Our service vehicles are fully stocked with common parts and diagnostic equipment. We quickly identify the problem, provide a clear explanation and upfront quote and complete repairs efficiently to minimize downtime.
