FrigTec is proudly led by Brandon, a Refrigeration Mechanic with over 12 years of industry experience. With a strong background in commercial refrigeration and air conditioning, FrigTec is committed to delivering reliable workmanship, professional service and quality solutions on every job.

From servicing and breakdowns to preventative maintenance and refrigeration requirements, we take pride in providing dependable results and building long-term relationships with our customers.

We're also committed to environmentally responsible refrigeration solutions through responsible refrigerant management, energy-efficient systems and staying current with industry standards and codes of practice.

Smarter Refrigeration. Lower Environmental Impact.
