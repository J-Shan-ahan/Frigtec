Expert Air Conditioning Installation

FrigTec provides professional commercial air conditioning installation services across Brisbane and the Gold Coast. Our experienced technicians design and install energy-efficient HVAC systems tailored to your business requirements.

What We Install

  • Split System Air Conditioners - Single and multi-head units for offices and retail spaces
  • Ducted Air Conditioning - Whole-building climate control solutions
  • VRV/VRF Systems - Variable refrigerant flow for large commercial buildings
  • Cassette Units - Ceiling-mounted systems for commercial spaces
  • Package Units - Rooftop and ground-mounted commercial HVAC

Our Installation Process

We begin with a comprehensive site assessment to understand your cooling requirements, building layout and budget. Our team designs an energy-efficient system that provides optimal comfort while minimizing operating costs. Every installation is completed to the highest standards with full compliance to Australian regulations.

Why Choose FrigTec for AC Installation?

  • ARCtick licensed and fully insured technicians
  • Energy-efficient system design to reduce power bills
  • Compliance with AS/NZS standards and building codes
  • Minimal disruption to your business operations
  • Comprehensive warranty on all installations
  • Post-installation support and maintenance plans

Installation Benefits

A professionally installed air conditioning system provides consistent comfort for your staff and customers, improves indoor air quality and operates efficiently to keep energy costs down. We ensure proper sizing, placement and configuration for maximum performance and longevity.
