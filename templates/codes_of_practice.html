Codes of Practice & Industry Standards

At FRIGTEC, we pride ourselves on staying up to date with evolving refrigeration codes of practice, Australian Standards and industry requirements.

Our focus is simple — delivering safe, compliant and future-focused commercial refrigeration and HVAC solutions that meet today's standards while preparing for tomorrow.
